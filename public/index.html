🛡️ Anti-Status Guard

Stops other members from getting away with posting a WhatsApp Group Status in groups you administer — deletes it for everyone, removes the sender, and posts your message. Also watches for a member posting their own personal status and mentioning/tagging the group as part of the audience: that status gets deleted every time, with a 3-strike warning before the sender is removed. Only ever acts in groups you turn this on for, and only where this account is currently a verified admin.

Before relying on this: detecting another member's incoming status post depends on how the WhatsApp library surfaces it, which couldn't be verified without a live connection while this was built. Turn it on, have someone (or a second number of your own) post a status in a small test group first, and check the Activity Log below and this group's entry in History to confirm it actually fired — don't assume it's catching everything until you've seen it work once.

Master switch

Status-mention guard (warns before kicking)
